## 1. Project Overview

LegalEase is a web-based legal document generator that helps users create basic legal document drafts.

## 2. Problem Statement

Manual preparation of legal documents can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are unfamiliar with legal formats.

## 3. Proposed Solution

LegalEase provides a structured web interface where users select a document type, enter information, and generate a legal document draft.

## 4. Supported Documents

### 1. Rental Agreement
Users can enter tenant, landlord, property, rent, rental period, and security deposit information.

### 2. Leave & License Agreement
Users can enter licensor, licensee, property, license period, and license fee information.

### 3. Affidavit
Users can enter declarant, address, purpose, and statement information.

### 4. Legal Notice
Users can enter sender, receiver, address, subject, and notice information.

## 7. User Guide

1. Open LegalEase.
2. Select a document type.
3. Enter the required information.
4. Generate the document.
5. Review the generated document.
6. Print or save the document.

## 8. Limitations

- AI-generated content requires review.
- Internet access may be required.
- AI service availability may affect generation.
- LegalEase does not provide professional legal advice.

## 10. Legal Disclaimer
