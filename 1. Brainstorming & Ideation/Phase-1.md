PHASE 1 – BRAINSTORMING & IDEATION

Project Title

LegalEase – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

---

1. Introduction

LegalEase is a web-based AI-powered legal document generation platform designed to help users create basic legal document drafts quickly and conveniently.

The system provides a simple interface where users can select the required type of legal document, enter relevant information, and generate a structured document draft.

The project aims to make basic legal document preparation more accessible to users who may not have extensive legal knowledge or experience with legal documentation.

«Note: LegalEase is intended to assist with drafting basic documents and does not replace professional legal advice.»

---

2. Problem Identification

Preparing legal documents manually can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are unfamiliar with legal terminology and document structures.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty understanding legal document formats.
- Time required to prepare documents manually.
- Lack of awareness of appropriate document structures.
- Repetitive writing of similar legal information.
- Difficulty organizing user-provided information into a formal document.
- Limited access to simple document-generation tools.

These challenges motivated the development of LegalEase.

---

3. Brainstorming

During the brainstorming stage, several ideas were considered for developing a practical AI-based application.

Possible ideas included:

1. AI-based resume generator.
2. AI-based educational assistant.
3. AI-based document summarizer.
4. AI-based legal document generator.
5. AI chatbot for general information.
6. AI-based form and letter generator.

After considering usefulness, feasibility, and real-world application, the team selected an AI-powered legal document generator.

---

4. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is LegalEase, a web-based application that simplifies the creation of basic legal document drafts.

The user can:

1. Open the LegalEase application.
2. Select the required document type.
3. Enter the necessary information.
4. Submit the information.
5. Allow the system to process the provided details.
6. Generate a structured legal document draft.
7. Review and use the generated document.

The planned architecture will use a web frontend connected to a FastAPI backend, with AI capabilities integrated into the document-generation workflow.

---

5. Project Objectives

The main objectives of LegalEase are:

- To simplify basic legal document drafting.
- To reduce the time required to create structured documents.
- To provide an easy-to-use web interface.
- To use AI to assist in generating appropriate document content.
- To organize user-provided information into a structured format.
- To maintain the existing four document types supported by the project.
- To develop a scalable backend using FastAPI.
- To provide a working web-based demonstration.
- To make the application accessible through the internet.

---

6. Target Users

LegalEase is designed primarily for:

- Students.
- Individuals preparing basic documents.
- Small organizations.
- Entrepreneurs.
- Users who need assistance preparing basic legal document drafts.
- People who want a simple starting point for organizing legal information.

The application is intended for basic drafting assistance and should not be treated as a substitute for a qualified legal professional.

---

7. Key Features

The planned key features of LegalEase include:

User-Friendly Interface

A simple web interface allows users to navigate the application easily.

Multiple Document Types

The application maintains support for the project's existing four legal document types.

AI-Assisted Generation

AI technology will assist in generating and structuring document content based on the information provided by the user.

FastAPI Backend

A FastAPI-based backend will handle application requests and connect the frontend with the document-generation and AI functionality.

Structured Documents

The generated content will follow an organized legal-document format.

Web Accessibility

The frontend can be hosted through GitHub Pages, while the backend can be deployed separately.

---

8. Innovation and Uniqueness

LegalEase combines a simple web interface with AI-assisted document generation.

The project focuses on making the initial drafting process easier by allowing users to provide information in a structured manner instead of manually preparing an entire document.

The planned separation between the frontend and FastAPI backend also provides a foundation for future improvements, such as:

- Additional document types.
- Improved AI-generated content.
- Document validation.
- Downloadable documents.
- User authentication.
- Improved document customization.

At the completion of the project, LegalEase is expected to provide:

- A FastAPI backend.
- AI-assisted document generation.
- Support for the project's four existing document types.
- A working connection between frontend and backend.

10. Project Vision

The vision of LegalEase is to create a simple and accessible AI-assisted platform that helps users prepare basic legal document drafts more efficiently.

The project aims to demonstrate how artificial intelligence and modern web technologies such as FastAPI can be combined to create a practical document-generation application.
